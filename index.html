How it works

From “yes” to a website, in four quiet steps.

  1. 01

    Tell your story

    Your names, the date, every function from Mehendi to Reception, families and travel — in a calm, guided form that saves as you go.

  2. 02

    Choose a design

    Pick from six premium designs, then tune colours, typography, layout, ornaments and animation in the Design Studio.

  3. 03

    Get your link

    Your website gets its own link with your names in it, a QR code and a ready-to-send WhatsApp message.

  4. 04

    Collect RSVPs

    Guests fill in a short form and their reply arrives on your WhatsApp or email — attendance, guest count and dietary notes.

Frequently asked questions

Questions, answered

Is it really free?+

Yes. There is no account, no database and nothing to pay. The website is static and hosted for free.

How long does it take?+

Most couples finish in 10–15 minutes. Your draft is saved in your browser as you type, so you can stop and continue later on the same device.

Where are our details stored?+

Inside your link. The part of the link after the # holds your website and is never sent to any server, so your details stay between you and the people you share it with.

Do guests need an account?+

No. Guests simply open your link, read everything and send their RSVP from their own WhatsApp or email.

Can I change things after sharing?+

Yes. Edit anything and you get a new link — send that one. People with the old link keep seeing the older version, so share the final link once you're happy.

Can I edit on another phone or laptop?+

Yes. Open your link, scroll to the bottom and press “Edit this website”. Everything opens in the builder again.

Where do RSVPs go?+

To the WhatsApp number or email you enter. Each reply is a ready-written message with the guest's name, attendance, number of guests, events and notes.

Do I need photographs?+

No. LUXE WED designs use elegant typography, line art and ornaments instead of photos.
